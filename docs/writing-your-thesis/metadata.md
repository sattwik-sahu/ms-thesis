# Setting Your Metadata

This is the **first file you should edit** — it controls your title page, certificate page, and committee listing everywhere in the document. Open:

```
meta/_info.tex
```

## What to fill in

```latex
\thesistitle{Your Thesis Title Here}
\studentname{Your Name}
\studentrollno{12345}
\subject{Your Department Name}
\degreename{Bachelor of Science -- Master of Science}
\supervisorname{Dr.\ Supervisor Name}
\supervisordesig{Supervisor Designation}
```

| Command | What it controls | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `\thesistitle{...}` | Title shown on the title page | `Learning Symbolic Representations for Robot Control` |
| `\studentname{...}` | Your full name | `John Smith` |
| `\studentrollno{...}` | Your roll number | `21000` |
| `\subject{...}` | Your department name | `Electrical Engineering and Computer Science` |
| `\degreename{...}` | Your degree — **leave as-is** unless you're on a different dual-degree program | `Bachelor of Science -- Master of Science` |
| `\supervisorname{...}` | Your supervisor's name, with title | `Dr.\ Jane Doe` |
| `\supervisordesig{...}` | Your supervisor's designation | `Associate Professor` |

!!! warning "Escaping the period after 'Dr'"
    Notice the backslash in `Dr.\ Supervisor Name`. In LaTeX, a period followed by a space is normally treated as the end of a sentence (which adds slightly more space). `\ ` forces a regular space instead, which is why the template writes it this way — keep this pattern when you edit it.

## Setting your review committee

Below the personal details, you'll find:

```latex
\setcommittee{
    First Member Name,
    Second Member Name,
    Third Member Name,
    Fourth Member Name,
}
```

Replace each entry with the name of a faculty member on your thesis review committee, one per line, separated by commas. You can add or remove members — the list isn't fixed to four names; the certificate page will automatically adjust to however many you list.

## How this connects to the rest of the thesis

Everything you set here is reused automatically:

- The **title page** (generated by `\maketitle` in `main.tex`) pulls your title, name, roll number, and degree.
- The **certificate page** (`meta/certificate.tex`) pulls your name, department, supervisor, and committee list.
- The **academic integrity disclaimer** also references your name and the date.

You only need to set these values **once** — you never have to retype your name or title anywhere else in the document.
